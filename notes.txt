HYPERTENSION: 5 KEY POINTS

1. Definition: Hypertension (high blood pressure) is defined as blood pressure consistently above 130/80 mmHg. It's often called the "silent killer" as it typically has no symptoms but significantly increases risk of heart disease, stroke, and kidney damage.

2. Risk Factors: Major risk factors include family history, age (risk increases with age), obesity, sedentary lifestyle, high sodium diet, excessive alcohol consumption, smoking, stress, and certain chronic conditions like diabetes.

3. Prevention/Management: Lifestyle modifications are crucial - maintaining healthy weight, regular physical activity (150 minutes/week), limiting sodium intake (<2300mg/day), following DASH diet (rich in fruits, vegetables, whole grains, lean proteins), limiting alcohol, avoiding tobacco, and managing stress.

4. Medication: When lifestyle changes aren't sufficient, medications may include diuretics, ACE inhibitors, ARBs, calcium channel blockers, and beta-blockers, often used in combination for optimal control.

5. Monitoring: Regular blood pressure monitoring is essential for diagnosis and management. Home monitoring provides valuable data, and regular healthcare provider visits help adjust treatment plans as needed.
